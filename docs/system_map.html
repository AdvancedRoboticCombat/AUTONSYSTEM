Nemesis Autonomy Map

How a camera frame becomes a wheel command, who is allowed to drive, and what goes quiet when something fails. Labels on the wires are ROS 2 topic names.

On the real robot

Who drives

The dashboard's OVERRIDE button flips /nemesis/override. The backend publishes it on a timer, so the switch still works if the camera drops out.

What happens when data stops

If our camera position or the IMU is more than 1 s old the policy stops publishing. If /control/channels is silent for 0.5 s the PPM output centres the sticks and stops the blade.

What is off until you ask

The policy always works out blade and arm commands, but they are only sent with scripts/all.sh --weapon; otherwise it drives only. The 6D pose tracker is parked, with its topics still subscribed.

Not wired yet

  • Arena points. HOMOG_DEST in main.py is empty, so no positions are published and the policy never starts until the arena points are entered and clicked in the dashboard.
  • Arena frame. The saw policy expects feet from a corner with turns counter-clockwise positive. If the arena points are entered differently, say so in saw_policy.yaml (units, origin, y_down).
  • Robot numbers. Body size, track width, latencies and arm timing in saw_policy.yaml are the simulator's nominal values, not measurements.
  • Weapon channels. Which transmitter input PPM channels 3 and 4 drive has not been checked, so blade and arm output is off by default.
  • IMU firmware. The receiver source now matches the transmitter's packet, but it has not been compiled or flashed.
